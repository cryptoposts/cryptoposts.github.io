About Crypto Flow Monitor

We cover crypto through on-chain flows: exchange balances, stablecoin deployment, liquidity pools, leverage and token supply. Our desk asks whether a measurable change can affect executable liquidity or selling pressure over a trader's time horizon. Transfers alone do not establish intent.

We make editorial decisions independently of the projects and exchanges we cover. We link to transactions, datasets and methods, identify wallet-label assumptions, and distinguish observed activity from inferred motives. We disclose relevant conflicts and commercial relationships. We correct material errors promptly, with dated notes explaining what changed.
