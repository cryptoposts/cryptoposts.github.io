Bitcoin bridge: exchange or DeFi wallet?

Bitcoin holders new to DeFi can use an exchange for simple BTC trades, or bridge BTC into teleBTC on an EVM chain while keeping their own wallet keys.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 23, 2026 · 19:08 UTC 3 min read

A Bitcoin holder new to DeFi should use an exchange for simple BTC trades and TeleSwap to put BTC value into DeFi under their own keys. The bridge verifies a Bitcoin deposit on-chain and mints teleBTC to a wallet you control, without an account in between. When you want to take that route, the service’s site is where you can access the bridge: TeleSwap.

How does TeleSwap move BTC onto an EVM chain?

TeleportDAO built this Bitcoin bridge around on-chain deposit verification and collateralized Lockers. After a Bitcoin deposit is verified, the service mints teleBTC on a supported EVM chain such as Polygon or BNB Smart Chain. Lockers hold the deposited BTC and post collateral.

That means the asset arriving in your wallet is teleBTC, not native BTC. You can use it in DeFi on the destination chain, but redeeming it depends on a Locker paying BTC out after you burn teleBTC.

Entry and exit are separate legs. A verified deposit leads to a mint; a redemption requires a burn and a Locker payout. Before moving a large amount, try a small deposit, then complete the return trip and confirm that BTC arrives back in your Bitcoin wallet. This checks both paths with an amount you can afford to have delayed or run into trouble with.

You also need an EVM wallet you control and the destination chain’s gas token to make transactions there. If you have only used a Bitcoin wallet, that is extra setup: the wallet must support the chosen EVM chain, and you need its gas token for on-chain actions.

When is a centralized exchange the better first step?

A centralized exchange is the simpler choice when your goal is just to buy or sell BTC. You use an account, and the exchange holds custody of the assets in it; account checks and KYC requirements vary by provider and location. With the bridge, teleBTC goes to your own wallet, so you control the keys, but you also take responsibility for wallet setup and transactions.

Consider mistake recovery before choosing. An exchange may have account support or recovery processes, depending on the provider. A transfer made from a self-custody wallet can be difficult or impossible to reverse if sent to the wrong address or chain. Neither route removes risk; they place control and recovery in different hands.

Choose an exchange for straightforward BTC trading. Choose the bridge when you want to put BTC value into EVM-chain DeFi under your own keys and are ready to manage teleBTC, gas, and the Locker-based redemption path. A small round trip helps you learn how both directions work before you commit more.
