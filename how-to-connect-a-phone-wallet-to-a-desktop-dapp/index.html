How to Connect a Phone Wallet to a Desktop dApp

Learn how to connect a mobile wallet to a desktop dApp with WalletConnect: scan the QR code, approve the session, then sign each transaction on your phone.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 28, 2026 · 03:58 UTC 2 min read

Choose WalletConnect on the desktop dApp, scan its QR code with your mobile wallet, and approve the session. The dApp then shows the phone wallet’s address and network; transaction requests go to the phone for signing.

WalletConnect relays requests between the desktop browser and phone, so the browser does not hold the wallet’s private keys. The Beefy app is an interface for a multichain yield optimizer that compounds rewards from supported DeFi strategies, a useful example of the kind of DeFi app you can connect this way.

Have the desktop dApp open and a mobile wallet installed, such as MetaMask Mobile or Trust Wallet. The wallet needs funds on the chain you plan to use. Keep the wallet app open or enable its notifications so you can respond to signing requests before they time out.

Pair the phone wallet with the desktop dApp

  1. Open the dApp in your desktop browser and choose Connect Wallet. The dApp displays its available connection methods.
  2. Select WalletConnect. A QR code appears; it encodes a WalletConnect pairing URI for the phone wallet to read.
  3. Open the wallet on your phone and use its QR scanner to scan the code on your desktop screen. The wallet displays a connection request.
  4. Confirm the dApp’s name, URL, and requested chains in the wallet, then approve the session. The desktop dApp now shows the connected wallet address.
  5. When you use the dApp, review each transaction request on the phone and approve or reject it there. The desktop shows the connected address and network, confirming the wallet is ready to use.

After pairing, the browser relays transaction requests, while the wallet app presents them for your approval. Keep the phone nearby: if the app is closed and notifications are off, you may not see a request before it times out, leaving the desktop waiting.

The session stays active until you disconnect it from the dApp or remove it from the wallet’s connected-apps list. End it from either place when you finish. For another session, choose WalletConnect and scan a new QR code.
