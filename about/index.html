About Block Signal

Block Signal covers the mechanics behind crypto markets: protocol releases, governance votes, token launches, security failures, and the on-chain activity that often moves before the headlines. Its focus is practical reporting over price-chasing.

The publication is edited by Mara Velez, a Chicago-based crypto journalist who spent eight years covering blockchain infrastructure and digital-asset policy for fintech trade publications. She has reported from developer conferences, reviewed protocol documentation, and interviewed engineers, researchers, and fund managers.

Block Signal separates reported facts from analysis and explains technical changes in plain language. The aim is a useful daily record for builders, investors, and curious readers who want to understand what changed before deciding what it means.
