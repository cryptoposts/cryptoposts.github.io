EU proposes crypto crackdown in 22nd Russia sanctions package

The European Commission’s 22nd Russia sanctions package would extend transaction bans to 60 banks and target crypto networks, including third-country enablers.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 26, 2026 · 17:39 UTC 2 min read

The European Commission proposed a new Russia sanctions package on 23 November that targets crypto and payment networks, including providers in third countries accused of helping Russia evade sanctions, according to the Associated Press report.

Commission President Ursula von der Leyen said the package would extend transaction bans to about 60 Russian banks and remove all 60 from the SWIFT international payments system. She also announced a Crypto Sanctions Task Force to support enforcement, according to the European Commission statement.

The proposed crypto measures focus on Russia and third countries that help it evade existing sanctions. Von der Leyen said the Commission wants to align efforts with international partners; the statement did not name specific crypto platforms or countries.

The package is the EU’s 22nd sanctions round against Russia. The Commission’s proposal also includes listing more than 70 ships from Russia’s shadow fleet and banning tanker vessels insured in Russia from EU ports, according to von der Leyen’s statement.

On sports, the Commission proposed barring Russian teams and athletes from competing under the Russian flag at events in the EU. It also proposed a ban on official Russian symbols at those events and restrictions on financial flows from Russian sponsors to European sports and cultural associations.

The measures are proposals, not sanctions already in force. They require approval from all 27 EU member states and could take weeks to negotiate, AP reported; the proposed package may change before adoption.
