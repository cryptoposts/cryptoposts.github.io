How Long Does It Take to Bridge Between Layer 2s? Plan Your Wait

How long does it take to bridge between Layer 2s? Fast relayer routes can settle in seconds to minutes; CCTP standard transfers take about 15–20 minutes.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 28, 2026 · 06:06 UTC 2 min read

Layer 2 bridge transfers usually take seconds to a few minutes on a fast relayer route, or around 15–20 minutes on a CCTP standard route. The difference is whether the route waits for the source chain to finalize on Ethereum.

  • Need the funds quickly? Compare routes that use a relayer fill, such as Across.
  • Sending USDC with CCTP standard? Allow around 15–20 minutes for finality.
  • Choosing between routes? Compare the estimated time shown in the quote before signing.

A bridge transfer starts when you send tokens on the source chain. The route then either uses a relayer to deliver the tokens on the destination chain or waits for source-chain finality before completing the transfer.

Across relayers can fill on the destination before the source transaction reaches finality. That can make the transfer faster, while the wait may vary with relayer liquidity.

Circle CCTP standard takes a finality-based path for transfers out of an L2. It waits for the source L2 to finalize on Ethereum before attestation, which is why the wait is typically around 15–20 minutes.

Say you want to move USDC from Arbitrum to Base. An Across route usually lands within a minute or two; a CCTP standard route takes around 15–20 minutes because it waits for Ethereum finality.

That timing difference matters when you need funds for a trade or payment on the destination chain. A fast fill can get them there sooner, while a finality-based route means you should plan for a longer wait.

The lifi bridge quote screen shows estimated time for each available route, so compare it before signing. The lifi bridge aggregator presents route options, which can help you choose between a faster Across fill and a CCTP standard transfer.

If relayer liquidity is low or the source chain takes longer to finalize, the wait can shift from the usual range. Recheck the route estimate before signing; a finality-based option may take longer than a fast relayer fill.
