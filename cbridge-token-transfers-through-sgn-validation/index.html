cBridge Token Transfers Through SGN Validation

cBridge tutorial for moving tokens between chains: connect your wallet, approve a supported token, confirm the transfer and check its arrival on the destination.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 27, 2026 · 16:16 UTC 2 min read

To move tokens between chains with cBridge, connect MetaMask, choose the source chain, destination chain and a supported token, approve it, confirm the transfer and check the destination balance. For the transfer itself, use cBridge to send a supported asset from one chain to another; the Celer bridge routes or pools the source token, and the State Guardian Network validates the transfer before payout.

How do you prepare, send and check a transfer?

Start with the wallet connected to the source chain, enough of that chain’s gas token for transactions, and a token and route supported on both chains. Celer’s official cross-chain transfer instructions describe the wallet approval and transfer flow.

  1. Select the source chain, destination chain and token. Ethereum, Arbitrum and BNB Chain are examples of EVM networks; confirm the token is available for your chosen route.
  2. Enter the amount. Review the estimated received amount and slippage setting before proceeding: the estimate is what you expect to land, and the amount can differ from what you send because of the bridge rate and fees.
  3. Approve the token in your wallet. This gives the bridge contract permission to use that token and requires a source-chain transaction. After approval, review the transfer details and confirm the transfer in your wallet.
  4. Wait for the source transaction and SGN confirmation, then check the token balance on the destination chain or its block explorer. The Celer security overview explains how SGN validators verify cross-chain messages before they are relayed.

Transfer history is tied to the connected wallet address. Reconnect the same address to find a pending transfer or request a refund if the transfer becomes eligible; a different address will not show that wallet’s history. Keep the source transaction hash handy if you need to trace its status.

How do refunds and bridge safety work?

How do I get a refund on the Celer bridge?

Reconnect the same wallet address and check its transfer history. If the transfer is marked as refundable, use the refund option there and confirm any resulting transaction in your wallet.

Is the Celer bridge safe to use?

SGN validation is part of the transfer’s security model, but it cannot remove every smart-contract, validator or route risk. Check the token and destination details before signing, and only proceed when the estimated amount and slippage setting are acceptable.
