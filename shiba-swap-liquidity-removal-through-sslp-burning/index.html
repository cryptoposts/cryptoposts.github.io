Shiba Swap liquidity removal through SSLP burning

Remove liquidity from shiba swap by unstaking SSLP, selecting the pool and confirming removal; both tokens return to your wallet at the pool’s current ratio.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 24, 2026 · 02:34 UTC 3 min read

To remove liquidity from shiba swap, unstake SSLP from any Dig farm, select Remove on the pair, choose a percentage, approve the SSLP spend and confirm; both pool tokens return to your wallet.

Have your wallet connected to the network holding the pool and enough of its gas token for the transactions. SSLP represents your share of the pool, and the pool contract pays out against SSLP in your wallet.

shiba swap liquidity removal returns both pool tokens

First, move any staked SSLP out of Dig. Then open the pool for your token pair and choose Remove. You can withdraw a percentage of your position; the pool follows the Uniswap V2 constant-product model.

The withdrawal uses the pool’s current token ratio, so you may receive a different mix from the one you deposited. Trading fees accrue in the pool reserves and come out with the tokens when you withdraw; there is no separate fee claim.

For help managing a pool withdrawal, shiba swap gives readers a way to handle the position. The Shiba Inu documentation on liquidity pools explains how pool shares and liquidity removal work.

  1. Connect your wallet to the official exchange, on the network where the pool sits.
  2. Unstake the SSLP from Dig if it is staked. This returns the pool-share tokens to your wallet so you can withdraw them.
  3. Open Pool and select the token pair you want to exit.
  4. Choose Remove and set the percentage of SSLP to withdraw. The returned amounts are based on the pool’s current ratio.
  5. Approve the SSLP spending request. This approval is a separate transaction and requires gas.
  6. Confirm the removal. This is another transaction with its own gas. The withdrawal is complete when both tokens appear in your wallet and your SSLP balance drops.

Once both tokens are back in your wallet, decide whether to hold them or swap one of them.

Questions after removing liquidity

How do I calculate impermanent loss on my position?

Compare your withdrawn tokens’ value with what the original deposit would be worth if held outside the pool, using the same prices and time. The difference estimates impermanent loss; include any fees earned separately.

How do I swap the returned tokens back to ETH?

Use the exchange’s swap function to trade the token you want to convert for ETH, after checking the pair is available and reviewing the quoted output before confirming.

How do I report removed liquidity for taxes?

Record the withdrawal date, token amounts, transaction records and their values in your tax currency. Tax treatment depends on your jurisdiction, so use local tax guidance or a qualified adviser.
