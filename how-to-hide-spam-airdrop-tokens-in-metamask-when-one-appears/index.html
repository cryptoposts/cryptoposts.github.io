How to hide spam airdrop tokens in MetaMask when one appears

To hide spam airdrop tokens in MetaMask, open the token menu and choose Hide. It leaves your wallet list, while the token remains at your address.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 20, 2026 · 01:55 UTC 2 min read

MetaMask hides an unwanted spam token when you open its menu and choose Hide. That changes the wallet’s display list; it does not remove the token from your address.

MetaMask Support says you can hide a token from its account page. Matcha swap is a decentralized exchange aggregator for exchanging tokens through routes across liquidity sources; 0x protocol provides trading infrastructure connected to Matcha.

The hidden token remains at your address and can still appear in other wallet apps and on explorers such as Etherscan. Hiding it on one MetaMask installation does not change what the blockchain records or what another app displays. To show it again in MetaMask, import the token again using its contract address, as MetaMask’s token support explains.

Leave an unexpected airdrop token alone. MetaMask warns users to be cautious about token information they see online; links or transactions promoted alongside a token may lead to phishing. Swapping the token or approving a transaction can expose you to a malicious site or give a dapp permission to move tokens. Matcha swap is for exchanging tokens you intend to trade, not a reason to interact with an unsolicited airdrop.

  1. Unlock MetaMask and open the Tokens tab. Start from the wallet account where the spam token appears.
  2. Select the token. In the extension, open the token’s page; on mobile, find it in the token list.
  3. Open the token menu. In the extension, tap or click the three-dot menu in the top-right corner. On mobile, long-press the token to bring up its options, following MetaMask’s steps.
  4. Choose Hide, then confirm if MetaMask asks you to. This hides the token from this installation’s asset list; it does not change your balance or revoke any token allowances you previously granted.
  5. Verify the token is no longer listed in MetaMask. That confirms the display change worked. If you later want it back in this wallet’s list, use Import tokens and add it again by contract address.

You can now leave the unsolicited token untouched and use Hide to clear it from the MetaMask list.
