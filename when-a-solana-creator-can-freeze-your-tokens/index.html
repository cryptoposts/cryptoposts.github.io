When a Solana Creator Can Freeze Your Tokens

Can a token creator freeze your tokens? On Solana, yes, if they still control the freeze authority, which can lock an individual token account until thawed.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 18, 2026 · 07:03 UTC 2 min read

On Solana, a token creator can freeze your token account only while they control its mint’s freeze authority, which can block transfers until the account is thawed.

A rugcheck can help you spot this control alongside other token risks. Our note on why a rugcheck finds a frozen balance can help when an account is already locked.

What happens when a creator freezes your account?

A Solana freeze authority can change a buyer’s token account to a frozen state. While frozen, the account cannot transfer or burn its tokens; the authority can thaw it later.

Say you buy a token and the creator still controls its freeze authority. They could freeze your token account, leaving the tokens there but preventing you from transferring them until the account is thawed.

The freeze applies to token accounts individually. Freezing your account does not, by itself, freeze every account holding that mint’s tokens.

The freeze authority is separate from the mint authority. The mint authority controls token creation; the freeze authority controls whether token accounts can transfer or burn. Creating a token alone does not give someone freeze power.

How can you check a token’s freeze authority?

For a Solana Program Library token, look up the exact mint address in Solscan and inspect its freeze authority field. The mint records this authority if one is set.

If the field names an authority, that authority can freeze token accounts for the mint. If it is unset, the freeze authority has been revoked for that mint. The mint authority may still be set; it governs token creation, not account freezes.

This check covers Solana SPL tokens. Other chains and token programs can use different controls, so confirm the chain and program before applying the same answer.

After checking the mint, compare its freeze authority with the account you hold and whether that authority remains active. That tells you whether your account can be frozen and what control to consider before transferring or buying.
