Native USDC reaches Arbitrum through a wallet bridge

How to move USDC from Polygon to Arbitrum without an exchange: connect MetaMask to a USDC bridge, send native USDC, and receive it at the same address.

Crypto Bulletin Newsroom Nov 28, 2026 · 05:02 UTC 2 min read

Move native USDC from Polygon to Arbitrum by signing a transfer from MetaMask; it arrives at the same wallet address. This route works when your Polygon balance is native USDC, not USDC.e.

A USDC bridge transfers tokens between blockchains while you keep control of your wallet and sign the transactions yourself. For background, this USDC bridge explanation shows how cross-chain transfers move tokens between networks.

What should you have ready?

Have MetaMask, POL on Polygon for gas, and Arbitrum added to your wallet. The POL covers the approval and transfer transactions.

Confirm which USDC token you hold before starting. Circle CCTP supports Polygon PoS and Arbitrum, and it burns native USDC on Polygon before minting USDC on Arbitrum. It cannot take USDC.e through that route: USDC.e is bridged USDC, not the native token CCTP burns.

Say your Polygon balance is USDC.e. Swap it for native USDC on Polygon first, where gas is cheap. Check the token contract before swapping so you select native USDC rather than a similarly named asset. Circle’s native USDC contract on Arbitrum is 0xaf88d065e77c8cC2239327C5EDb3A432268e5831.

Circle CCTP is one transfer mechanism; Across Protocol is another bridge name you may encounter. Check the selected route and token representation before signing to confirm it sends native USDC to Arbitrum.

How do you move the USDC?

Use the wallet connected to Polygon and select Polygon as the source and Arbitrum as the destination. The transfer should return native USDC to the same address on the destination chain.

  1. Swap USDC.e to native USDC on Polygon if needed. The wallet should show native USDC as the balance you plan to send.
  2. Open a USDC bridge and connect MetaMask. The bridge should show your connected wallet address.
  3. Select Polygon to Arbitrum and USDC to USDC. Confirm the displayed source token is native USDC.
  4. Enter the amount to transfer. The bridge should show the amount and destination address before you proceed.
  5. Sign the approval in MetaMask. The approval lets the transfer use the specified USDC amount.
  6. Review the transfer details, then sign the transfer. MetaMask submits it on Polygon; save the transaction hash so you can track the transaction.
  7. Switch MetaMask to Arbitrum and check the USDC balance at Circle’s contract address above. When the balance appears, the USDC is in your wallet on Arbitrum.
