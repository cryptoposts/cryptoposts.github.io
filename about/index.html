About Block Today

Block Today is an independent newsroom covering crypto: exchanges, protocols, stablecoins, regulation, enforcement and security incidents. We report what happened, who was involved and the figures that matter, then add only the context needed to read it.

No project we cover owns or pays us. Every story is sourced to filings, on-chain records, official statements or named reporting, and errors are corrected on the story with a note. We are independent, and nothing here is financial advice.
